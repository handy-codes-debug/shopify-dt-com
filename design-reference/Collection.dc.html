Choosing a graphics card

Match the card to your monitor's resolution and refresh rate first, then check three things: the card's length against your case, its recommended PSU wattage, and the power connector it needs. Not sure? Send us your parts list and we'll confirm compatibility. Read the GPU buying guide.
